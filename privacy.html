Privacy

Last updated July 12, 2026.

Information we use

The local account service stores the email, display name, password hash, membership records, LottoCredits ledger, and game reward history needed to provide the signed-in experience. Passwords are never stored in plain text.

Browser storage

Saved stories, preferences, demo state, and interface settings may be stored in your browser. Clearing site data removes those browser-only records.

Payments

Stripe processes test and future live checkout information. LOTTOMINDED ULTRA does not place Stripe secret keys or full payment-card data in frontend code.

News and external links

News cards link to original publishers. External sites apply their own privacy and cookie policies.

Contact

Questions can be sent through the contact page.

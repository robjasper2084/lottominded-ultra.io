Accessibility

LOTTOMINDED ULTRA is being improved for keyboard, screen-reader, touch, reduced-motion, and high-contrast use.

Current support

  • Skip links and semantic page landmarks.
  • Visible keyboard focus for interactive controls.
  • Reduced-motion support for animated surfaces.
  • Text alternatives for meaningful images and labels for controls.
  • Responsive layouts with large touch targets.

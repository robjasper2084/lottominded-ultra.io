Drop-Down Manual

Instruction Manual - Quick Start
  1. Open Direct to launch the full studio, or use the standalone prompt apps.
  2. Load audio you own, create a beat, or type a session seed.
  3. Use Play All, Pause, Restart, Stop All, Loop, Loop All, synced decks, and record mix to perform the session.
  4. Generate Suno prompts, video prompts, and Beat2Lotto+™ number signals from the same creative seed.
Beat2Lotto+™ Audio Import Manual
  1. Open Beat2Lotto+™ and import a browser-supported audio file.
  2. The app analyzes duration, peak, RMS, zero crossings, transient activity, and an energy signature locally.
  3. Choose Pick 3, Pick 4, Cash 5, Lotto 6, Powerball-style, or Mega Millions-style output.
  4. Generate beat-seeded entertainment picks. These are creative number generations, not predictions.
  5. Verify game rules, matrices, draw times, prizes, and eligibility with official lottery sources before playing.
Sheet Music + Keyboard Prompt Manual
  1. Upload a sheet image or PDF in Beat2Lotto+™.
  2. Preview the sheet locally, then confirm the melody with the on-screen keyboard or editable note lane.
  3. Generate a music prompt from the note phrase, BPM, and session seed.
  4. Generate a video prompt that turns the notes into camera motion, glowing staff lines, and beat-synced visuals.
Safety + Rights Manual

Use audio, sheet music, samples, and references you own or have permission to process. The website does not upload audio, submit prompts to outside tools, sell tickets, or process wagers.
